## Full description

One tap. One sharp turn. How long can you stay on the road?

ZigZag Rush is a fast, easy-to-learn arcade driving game built around precise timing. Tap to change direction, turn close to the centre of each tile to grow a PERFECT combo, and collect gems while the road becomes faster and more demanding.

FEATURES

- Responsive one-touch controls
- PERFECT turns, score multipliers, and combo chasing
- Speed boosts, slow-motion tiles, shields, gem magnets, moving roads, crumbling tiles, and mystery rewards
- Daily challenges with a new target each day
- Daily missions and milestone achievements
- Six car colours with matching neon trails
- Three unlockable visual worlds
- Optional rewarded ads for a revive or extra gems
- No forced banner ads
- Offline gameplay; an internet connection is only needed for ads

Challenge yourself, improve your best score, unlock every style, and own the endless road.
