# Third-party asset release audit

## Evidence present in the project

- AurynSky Gems Ultimate Pack: `Assets/AurynSky/Gems Ultimate Pack/READ ME.txt` explicitly permits commercial-project use.
- TextMesh Pro Liberation Sans font: its OFL license is included at `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts/LiberationSans - OFL.txt`.
- TextMesh Pro EmojiOne attribution is included at `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Sprites/EmojiOne Attribution.txt`.
- External Dependency Manager for Unity includes its license at `Assets/MobileDependencyResolver/Editor/LICENSE`.
- The app icon and feature graphic in `StoreAssets` were generated specifically for this project in this development session.

## Release blockers requiring owner records

- Music tracks `JumpyGame.wav` and `Tiny_Blocks.wav`: add the purchase/download source and commercial distribution license.
- Sound effect `693840__nxrt__collecting-coins (mp3cut.net) (1).wav`: add the original source URL, author, license, and any required attribution. The filename alone is not sufficient evidence.
- Car model, materials, and any imported fonts outside TextMesh Pro: retain marketplace receipts or source licenses.

Do not publish until the files above have adequate commercial-use evidence. If evidence cannot be found, replace the asset with one having a clear license.
