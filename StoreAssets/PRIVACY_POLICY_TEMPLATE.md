## Information stored on the device

ZigZag Rush stores gameplay progress locally on the device, including high score, gem balance, unlocked and selected cosmetic items, daily challenge and mission progress, tutorial status, and music, haptic, and advertising choices. The game does not currently create user accounts or send this local progression data to the developer's own servers.

## Advertising

The game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation to offer rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. Advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. The exact partners and data practices depend on the ad networks enabled in the LevelPlay dashboard.
